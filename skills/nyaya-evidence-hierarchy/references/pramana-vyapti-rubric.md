# Pramāṇa and vyāpti decision rubric

## Fast classification questions

1. Does the source itself report a measurement/observation of the exact phenomenon relevant to this claim? If yes, record **pratyaksha** for that observation.
2. Does the claim add a model, latent mechanism, statistical conclusion, or logical step beyond the observation? If yes, record **anumana**, naming its premises and assumptions.
3. Is the claim transferred from another system because of likeness? If yes, record **upamana**, with similarities and differences.
4. Does the source state it without data or substantive reasoning in that source? Record **shabda**, even if it cites elsewhere. Trace cited support separately if in scope.

A single passage may produce more than one evidence record for separate claims. If the basis cannot be inspected, use `unassessed`/`unresolved` rather than guessing.

## Boundary cases

- An I-V trace: pratyaksha for current behavior, not automatically for a filament mechanism.
- A fitted transport model: anumana for the model conclusion, even if its input curve is direct.
- A review repeating a primary-paper mechanism: shabda in the review; evaluate the primary paper separately.
- Similar behavior in a related composition: upamana unless the relationship is directly measured in the target composition.
- A textbook law cited without derivation: shabda within that document, but it may refer to a separately registered vyapti anchor.

## Vyāpti anchor review checklist

An anchor entry must state a precise proposition, domain, conditions, authoritative foundations, apparent counterexamples, and an upadhi assessment. Reject elevation based on citation count, repetition, or a short series of compatible experiments. Scope is mandatory: a universal-looking statement may only be an invariant in a specified regime.

## Reporting language

Say “directly observed”, “inferred under assumptions”, “supported by analogy”, or “asserted in the source” rather than using hierarchy labels alone. Do not claim that an anchor proves a mechanism merely because the mechanism is compatible with it.
